# Stylized Nature MegaKit (Standard)

By Quaternius (https://quaternius.com), CC0 1.0 (public domain): see LICENSE.txt. No credit required; it's listed
in the game's Credits anyway.

- `Models/`: the kit's "FBX (Unity)" models, unchanged.
- `Textures/`: the colour textures the models use, scaled to 256 px for the pixel look and the web build, and named
  after the kit's material names (the leaf maps are the kit's `_C` variants). The normal maps aren't used.
