# ------------------------------------------------------------------ Battle tab

[battle] Your turn
Each turn: {gold}plan{/c}, then press {gold}Go{/c}.

You get {gold}1 AP{/c} per fighter standing ({gold}3{/c} with a full party).

Each card played costs {gold}1 AP{/c} and waits in the {gold}queue{/c} at the top.

{gold}Click the queue{/c} to take the whole turn back.

[battle] Cards
Your hand holds {gold}AP + 4{/c} cards and refills every turn.

A card's colour shows whose it is. Its top-right icon shows what it does: {gold}Attack{/c}, {gold}Buff{/c}, {gold}Debuff{/c}, {gold}Heal{/c} or {gold}Counter{/c}.

{gold}Hold{/c} a card to read it. Hover one to see who it hits.

[battle] Merging
Two {gold}identical{/c} cards side by side merge into one card a {gold}rank higher{/c} (top: {gold}III{/c}).

Merges happen by themselves when cards are drawn or played.

{gold}Drag{/c} a card to line one up ({gold}1 AP{/c}). Playing the card in between is free.

Each merge: {gold}+1 Ult{/c} for its owner.

[battle] Ult
Each fighter has {gold}5 Ult pips{/c} under their HP bar.

{gold}+1{/c} per card of theirs played, {gold}+1{/c} per merge.

Full pips: their {gold}Ult card{/c} joins your hand ({gold}1 AP{/c}).

Ult resets after every battle. Enemies have Ult too.

[battle] Targets
{gold}Click an enemy{/c} to target it (the red lock-on). Single-target cards hit your target.

The small card above an enemy is its {gold}intent{/c}: its move this turn.

[battle] Resonance
Some resonances (elements) beat others:

{gold}Electric{/c} > {gold}Fire{/c} > {gold}Toxin{/c}
> {gold}Earth{/c} > {gold}Electric{/c}
{gold}Light{/c} and {gold}Void{/c} beat each other.
{gold}Physical{/c} is always neutral.

Advantage: {green}+30% damage, +15% crit{/c}
Disadvantage: {red}-20% damage{/c}

[battle] Statuses
The icons under a unit are its {gold}statuses{/c}.

The corner number is the total of all stacks.

{gold}Hover{/c} one for its name, what it does and the turns left.

[battle] Inspect
{gold}Hold{/c} a unit (or ring it and press {gold}Space{/c}) to see its HP, stats, resonance and cards.

Check enemy cards before a big fight.

A click, {gold}Space{/c} or {gold}Esc{/c} closes it.

[battle] Party and backup
Up to {gold}3{/c} fight. A {gold}4th{/c} waits as {gold}backup{/c} and steps in when one falls.

Fewer fighters: less AP, smaller hand.

Everyone down: you wake at the last {gold}Access Point{/c}. Rest there to heal.

[battle] Speed and pause
{gold}Tab{/c}: turn speed {gold}1x{/c} / {gold}2x{/c} / {gold}4x{/c}.

{gold}Esc{/c} or {gold}P{/c}: pause, with Settings (and these tutorials), Restart Fight and Leave Fight.
