# Right after she joins: "want a quick rundown?" (the last line asks; the answers are tutorial.offer.options).
[tutorial.offer]
Erin | Something's moving out there. A machine, a small one.
Erin | Want a quick rundown before we fight together?

[tutorial.offer.options]
Yes, show me.
I know how.

[tutorial.skip]
Erin | Then let's go. I'll follow your lead.

[tutorial.erin.intro]
Erin | Okay! Fighting together's all about the cards. Every card costs 1 AP, and we get 1 AP each per turn.

# Step 1: tap a Physical card.
[tutorial.erin.1]
Erin | First, tap a Physical card. That one, glowing. It costs 1 AP.

# Step 2: move a card next to its match so they merge into Rank II.
[tutorial.erin.2]
Erin | See those two matching cards? Spend 1 AP to slide one next to the other, and they'll merge into Rank II.

# Out of AP: press Go.
[tutorial.erin.go]
Erin | We're out of AP. Press Go and watch the cards play out.

# Step 3: cast the Rank II card.
[tutorial.erin.3]
Erin | Now cast the Rank II card. Merged cards hit harder, and their Break power is bigger too. That grey bar under its health? Empty it.

[tutorial.erin.go_break]
Erin | That's it. Press Go, and hit that guard!

# Step 4: BREAK, then the finishing strike.
[tutorial.erin.4]
Erin | {shake}BREAK!{/shake} It's stunned and loses its turn, and every hit on it does extra damage while it's down.
Erin | Finish it! Play anything you like, then Go.

[tutorial.erin.done]
Erin | {wave}Nice!{/wave} You've got it. From here on, it's all you.
