# A3: sprite credits

A3 is built from two pieces of A2 (*NieR:Automata*) art, neither of them licensed for this project; the game is a
free, non-commercial fan project and the rights holders' names are kept here.

- **Body:** an A2 pixel-art study (`Tools/ChibiForge/reference/A3_study.gif`). Artist: TODO (find and add the study's
  creator). It is reworked for the game: the sword removed (`a3_portrait.py`), a breathing and blinking idle
  (`a3_study.py`), sturdier legs and a full outline (`a3_model.sturdy`), a black blindfold, three rigged walks
  (`a3_walks.py`), and every battle clip (attack, cast, hurt) posed from it with the walk rig (`a3_walks.hold`).
- **Hair:** six hair-sway frames from A2's unit sheet in **Last Cloudia** (the NieR:Automata collab), (c) **Aidis Inc. /
  Square Enix**, saved once as hair only (`Tools/ChibiForge/reference/A3_hair.png`) and laid on the study by
  `a3_oldhair.py`. Nothing else from that sheet is used any more; the sheet itself is out of the project
  (2026-11-05).

The walks' timing: **Reference** is keyed from the *8-frame pixel art walk cycle template* by MostlyDecentGames
(https://mostlydecent.itch.io/8-frame-walk, CC0), timing and poses only. **Gandalf** is inspired by the walk in the
*GandalfHardcore Character Asset Pack* (https://gandalfhardcore.itch.io), timing studied by eye only. No pixels from
either are used.

Party colours come from Lospec palettes: see `Tools/ChibiForge/palettes/README.md`.
`portrait.png` (the Esc menu's party page, Soul cards and Ult pictures) is the study with its own hair.
